# Why VolunteerDB

A parish is a place people pass through. A family moves for work, a student
graduates, an older parishioner moves closer to a daughter. Each of them
served somewhere, and often in more than one place. The priest's question is
always the same: when this parishioner leaves, what holes do I have to fill?
VolunteerDB exists to answer that question from one screen, in a minute.

## What the site keeps

The site keeps 5 kinds of fact. It keeps the people: a name, an email
address, a phone number, a photo. It keeps the teams, about 50 of them, one
for each ministry; some hold smaller teams, for example the greeters inside
hospitality. It keeps each
person's role on each team: *Ministry leader*, *Second-in-command*, *Core
team member* or *Member*. It keeps the events and their shifts, with who
agreed to serve and who covered for whom. And it keeps the history, so a
change is never lost.

## Where the answer appears

Open a volunteer's page and the section *If they leave, what vacancies
appear?* lists every team that person serves on. A team left with no leader
and no second is marked in red; one left with only a second is marked in
amber. The *Dashboard* asks the same question for the whole parish under
*Needs attention*. It counts the teams *Without a leader*, the teams
*Without a second*, and the *Shifts short of people*. The choir has 30
singers and one director; the site shows that the director is the hole, not
the singers.

## Why not a spreadsheet in the office

A spreadsheet shows one team at a time, and it forgets what it showed last
year. One parishioner who serves on 4 teams appears on 4 sheets, and nobody
adds them up. VolunteerDB shows the whole parish at once, so the same person
is counted once, and it remembers every past roster. A leader who likes the
spreadsheet keeps it: the site can read a team's Google Sheet every night.
See [Spreadsheets and home pages](spreadsheets-and-home-pages.md).

## What the site is not

VolunteerDB is not a mailing list. It sends a short notice when something
needs your attention, and nothing else. A sign-in code, a shift reminder, a
call for a substitute: that is the whole of it. It is not a payroll or an account book:
it holds no money and no donations. It is not a directory of every
household; it lists the people who serve. It keeps what a coordinator needs
to fill a seat, and no more.
