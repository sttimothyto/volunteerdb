# Words

The words you meet in the app, 1 line each.

## People and teams

- **Volunteer** — a person on the parish's list: name, contact details, notes, and the teams they serve on. A volunteer can exist with no account.
- **Team** — a ministry, for example *Liturgy* or *Hospitality*. Every team has a roster.
- **Sub-team** — a team inside another team, for example *Music Ministry* inside *Liturgy*. The leaders of the parent team lead the sub-team too.
- **Roster** — the list of the people on a team, with the role of each.
- **Role** — what a person is on 1 team: leader, second, core member or member. The same person can hold a different role on each team.
- **Leader** — *Ministry leader*: the person who runs the team, its roster, its events and its elections.
- **Second** — *Second-in-command*: a second leader with the same rights as the leader.
- **Core member** — *Core team member*: a trusted member who can see the whole roster with contact details, but does not manage it.
- **Member** — *Member*: a person on the team who sees the names on the roster, but no contact details.
- **Administrator** — a person whose account can do everything on the site, on every team.
- **No team** — a volunteer on no roster at all. The *Dashboard* counts them under *On no team*.

## Service

- **Event** — an occasion when a team serves: a Mass, a work day, a fundraiser. It has a start, an end, a place and slots.
- **Slot** — a named position at an event, for example *Lector*, with a number of places.
- **Shift** — your place in a slot at 1 event: the thing you sign up for, or a leader schedules you for.
- **Substitute** — a teammate who takes over your shift when you can no longer serve.
- **RSVP** — your answer to *Can you serve at this event?*: *Available* or *Not available*. It is an answer, not a commitment; the shift is the commitment.
- **Task force** — a temporary team that the site makes when 2 teams staff 1 event together. It holds both rosters, for that event only, and goes away within an hour after the event ends.
- **Workload band** — the colour a volunteer's workload score falls in: *green*, *amber* or *red* as standard. Only leaders, seconds and administrators see it.

## Elections

- **Election** — the way a team fills an empty seat: candidates are nominated, a roll votes, and a leader appoints. The seat is usually the leader's or the second's.
- **Seat** — the role on 1 team that an election is about, usually the leader or the second.
- **Roll** — the list of the people who can vote in 1 election. They and the team's leaders can nominate.
- **Ballot** — your scores for the candidates, 0 to 5 each. Nobody sees your scores; only the totals are shown.

## The site

- **Home page** — a team's public page, made from a Google Doc, that anyone on the internet can read without a sign-in.
- **Roster spreadsheet** — a team's Google Sheet, kept in step with the roster every night. See [The roster spreadsheet](roster-spreadsheet.md).
- **As-of date** — a date in the past. With an as-of date set, a page shows the parish as it was on that day, under the banner *Read-only snapshot as of …*.
- **Invite** — the link a new account holder gets by email to open their account. It works once, and only for a limited time.
- **Sign-in code** — the 6-digit code the site emails you when you sign in without a password. It works for 10 minutes.
- **Account** — your sign-in: an email address, with or without a password. It is linked to your volunteer record.
