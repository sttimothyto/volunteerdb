# The roles

What each kind of person can see and do. A role is held on 1 team, and a leader, second or core member of a team holds the same role on its sub-teams. A person can hold a different role on each team.

## Everyone who is signed in

- Sees the *Dashboard*, with *My teams* and *My service* when their account is linked to a volunteer.
- Sees the *Teams* page: every team, but only the names and the tree.
- Sees the *Volunteers* page and can search it. Contact details of people outside their teams show as *•••*.
- Sees their own profile and can edit their own contact details.
- Sees the *Events* page. The table holds only the events of their own teams; the *Whole parish* calendar shows every team's events.
- Can add, replace or remove the photo of any volunteer.
- Sees *Your account* and can set, change or remove their own password and change their own email address.
- Can subscribe their own calendar to their duties, and to the whole parish's events.
- Sees the manual and the public ministry pages.

## No team

- A volunteer on no roster has everything in the list above and nothing more.
- Their *Events* table is empty, and they see no roster.

## Member

- Sees the names on the roster of their own team, with each person's role and whether they have an account.
- Does not see the email addresses, phone numbers or notes of the people on that team.
- Sees the events of that team. Can sign up, answer *Can you serve at this event?*, ask for a substitute, hand a shift over, and take a teammate's open shift.
- The member role does not reach the sub-teams of that team.

## Core member

- Everything a member can do, on the team and its sub-teams.
- Sees the full roster with email addresses and phone numbers.
- Sees the full profile of every person on those teams, with custom fields.
- Can export the roster of those teams as a `.csv` file, with the notes column left blank.
- Can copy the team's email list, and email the whole team.
- Can send a person on those teams an invitation to create an account.
- Can set or change the team's public home page.
- Sees *Needs attention* on the *Dashboard* with 3 figures: the teams they help run, the people on them, and the people without an email address.
- Does not see the leadership gaps, the workload bands, the shifts short of people or the open seats.
- Does not edit anyone else's contact details, and does not see workload scores.

## Leader and second

- Everything a core member can do, on the team and its sub-teams. The 2 roles have the same rights.
- Manages the roster: adds a person, changes a role, removes a person.
- Edits the contact details, notes and custom fields of the people on those teams.
- Sees the workload band and score of the people on those teams, on the *Volunteers* page, on profiles, and on the *Dashboard*.
- Creates, edits and cancels the team's events, adds and removes slots, schedules people, and records attendance.
- Adds a collaborating team to an event, which makes a task force.
- Sees *Elections*, starts a proposal for an empty seat on those teams, edits its deadlines and roll, appoints, cancels, and starts a new round.
- Links a roster spreadsheet, syncs it, and imports a `.csv` file for those teams.
- Sees the coverage columns and the *Gaps* column on the *Teams* page, and the leadership gaps on the *Dashboard*.
- Gets the email when a member takes themselves off a shift.
- A leader of a task force manages that event only, not the people the task force borrowed.

## Voter

- A voter is a person on the roll of 1 election. The roll starts with the team's leader, second and core members, plus the *Clergy* team.
- Sees *Elections* in the header while on a roll, and keeps it after the decision to see the result.
- Sees that election's page: the candidates, the roll, and the result once voting closes.
- Can nominate a candidate while nominations are open.
- Can cast a ballot, and change it, while voting is open. To vote, the voter needs an account linked to their volunteer record.
- Nobody sees the scores on a ballot, not even an administrator.

## Administrator

- Everything above, on every team, whether or not they are on it.
- Creates, edits, archives and deletes teams, and sets each team's workload weight.
- Creates and deletes volunteers, and marks a volunteer active or inactive.
- Exports the whole parish as 1 file.
- Sees *Accounts*, *Fields* and *Workload* in the header: creates accounts, makes administrators, switches accounts off, links them, sends invitations, and sees the invitation link itself.
- Defines the custom fields and the workload bands.
- Changes the site logo by a click on it.
- Sees the *Parish* figures on the *Dashboard*, and the warning when the site is close to its email limit.
