# The emails the site sends

Every email the site sends, with its subject line, who gets it, when, and what to do with it. Every message comes from the site's own address, and none of them asks you for a password.

## Your VolunteerDB account

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB account*, followed by *at* and the parish's name when the site has one.
- Who gets it: a person for whom an account was just created, or whose invitation was sent again.
- When: at once, when an administrator, a leader, a second or a core member sends the invitation.
- What to do: open the link, choose a password or skip it, tick the confidentiality box, and sign in. The link works once, and only for a limited time, usually 7 days. If it ran out, sign in with an emailed code instead.

## Your VolunteerDB sign-in code

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB sign-in code:* followed by the 6-digit code.
- Who gets it: the person who typed their email address on the sign-in page and left the password blank.
- When: at once. If you ask again within 1 minute, the site does not send a second code; wait for the first.
- What to do: type the code on the sign-in page within 10 minutes. If you did not ask for it, ignore it; your account is safe.

## Your VolunteerDB account is ready

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB account is ready*.
- Who gets it: a person who just finished the setup from their invitation link.
- When: at once.
- What to do: nothing. It says how you sign in from now on: with a password, or with an emailed code.

## Your VolunteerDB password changed

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB password changed*.
- Who gets it: the owner of the account, at the account's address.
- When: at once, when you set, change or remove your password on *Your account*. The first password, chosen from the invitation link, brings the message above instead.
- What to do: nothing if that was you. If it was not, tell the parish office straight away: somebody else has your account.

## Confirm your new VolunteerDB address

- Subject: *Confirm your new VolunteerDB address*.
- Who gets it: the new address, when somebody asks to move an account to it.
- When: at once.
- What to do: open the link and click *Confirm this address* if you asked for the change. The link works once, for 24 hours. If you ask again, the earlier link stops working. If you did not ask, ignore it; nothing happens without the link.

## Your VolunteerDB address is being changed

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB address is being changed*.
- Who gets it: the old address of the account, the moment the change is asked for.
- When: at once, together with the message above.
- What to do: nothing if that was you. If it was not, sign in, click *Cancel* on *Your account*, and tell the parish office.

## Your VolunteerDB address changed

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB address changed*.
- Who gets it: the old address, once the new one is confirmed. It is the last message to that address.
- When: at once.
- What to do: nothing if that was you. If it was not, tell the parish office straight away.

## Your VolunteerDB address was changed

- Subject: *Your VolunteerDB address was changed*.
- Who gets it: the old address of a volunteer whose address a leader, second or administrator corrected.
- When: at once. It is the last message to that address.
- What to do: nothing if you knew about it. The address you sign in with does not change. If it is news to you, tell the parish office.

## VolunteerDB: your upcoming service

- Subject: *VolunteerDB: your upcoming service*.
- Who gets it: a volunteer with a shift, 1 message a night at most, whatever the number of shifts.
- When: early in the morning after a leader schedules you. If you asked for reminders at sign-up, it comes again about 7 days before and about 1 day before the event. The day-before reminder is on unless you switched it off. If you signed up yourself, or took a teammate's shift, you get the reminders only.
- What to do: put the date in your diary. If you can no longer serve, open *Events* and ask for a substitute.

## Substitute needed

- Subject: *Substitute needed:* followed by the event's name.
- Who gets it: every member of the event's team who is not already serving at it.
- When: at once, when a teammate asks for a substitute. A team can send at most 6 of these a day; after that the request still shows on the *Events* page, without an email.
- What to do: if you can take the shift, open *Events* and click *Take this slot*. The first to claim it gets it.

## Substitute found

- Subject: *Substitute found:* followed by the event's name.
- Who gets it: the person who asked for the substitute.
- When: at once, when a teammate claims the shift.
- What to do: nothing. The schedule is updated.

## You're now serving

- Subject: *You're now serving:* followed by the event's name.
- Who gets it: the teammate a shift was handed to directly.
- When: at once.
- What to do: put the date in your diary. If it does not suit you, open *Events* and pass the shift on or ask for a substitute.

## Off the roster

- Subject: *Off the roster:* followed by the event's name.
- Who gets it: the leaders and seconds of the event's team.
- When: at once, when a member takes themselves off a shift. The message quotes their reason.
- What to do: open the event and schedule somebody else if the slot needs a person.

## Cancelled

- Subject: *Cancelled:* followed by the event's name.
- Who gets it: everyone who held a shift at the event.
- When: at once, when a leader cancels an event that has not ended yet.
- What to do: nothing.

## VolunteerDB elections: your input is needed

- Subject: *VolunteerDB elections: your input is needed*.
- Who gets it: a person on the roll of an election, 1 message a night at most.
- When: early in the morning after you were put on a roll, and early in the morning after voting opens. Both deadlines are in the message.
- What to do: sign in, open *Elections*, and nominate a candidate or cast your ballot before the deadline. A deadline day counts to its end.

## The site's own alerts

- Subject: *[volunteerdb] nightly*, the name of the job, and *FAILED*; or *[volunteerdb]*, the name of the job, and *FAILED*.
- Who gets it: the person who runs the site, at the address set up for alerts. Nobody else.
- When: when a nightly job, for example the roster sync or the reminders, fails; at most once a day for each job.
- What to do: nothing, unless you run the site.
