# Cover an open shift

Take over a slot that a teammate can no longer fill.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and you are a member of the team of the event.
- You are not already at that event, and the request is not your own.
- The first teammate to claim the slot takes it.
- An email, *Substitute needed:* followed by the name of the event, tells you that a teammate asked. The note of your teammate is in it.

## Steps

1. Click *Dash* in the header.
2. Under *My service*, click the tile *Shifts I could cover*. Or click *Events* in the header.
3. Find the section *Teammates need a substitute*.
4. Read the line: the name of your teammate, the slot, the event, the time and the note.
5. Click *Take this slot*.

## What you see

- The page says *The slot is yours — thank you!*
- The event appears under *Your upcoming duties*. On the event page, your name is on the slot with the badge *substitute*.
- Your teammate gets an email, *Substitute found:* followed by the name of the event.
- You get a reminder email the day before the event.
- The same request is on the event page, under *Substitutes wanted*, with the same button.
- The tile *Shifts I could cover* is on the *Dashboard* only while a request waits for you.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says *someone else already claimed this slot*, a teammate was faster. Nothing changes for you.
- If the page says *you already serve at this event*, you hold a slot there already.
- If the page says *only members of the event's team can take part*, you are not on that team.
- If the page says *cannot claim this slot: event has already ended*, the event is past.
- If you cannot serve after all, open the event page. Click *Need a sub*, *Hand off* or *Withdraw* next to your name.
