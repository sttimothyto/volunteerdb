# Run an election

Fill a leader or second-in-command seat by nomination and vote.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of the team, or of a team above it.
- Read [Elections](../explanation/elections.md) first. It says who votes, how the votes are counted, and why the result is advice.

## Steps

### Start a proposal

1. Open *Elections*.
2. Under *Vacancies*, find the team and click *Start proposal*.
3. Check *Role*. It is set to the seat that is empty.
4. Click *First candidate* and pick a name. Say why in *Why them?*
5. Set *Nominations close (YYYY-MM-DD)* and *Voting closes (YYYY-MM-DD)*. The defaults are 2 and 4 weeks from today.
6. Say what the seat is, and why now, in *Notes (what is this seat, and why now?)*
7. Click *Create proposal*.

### While nominations are open

- Anyone on the roll can add a candidate: pick a name in *New candidate*, fill in *Why them?*, click *Nominate*.
- Under *Voting members*, you can add a voter (*Add a voter*, then *Add voter*) or *Remove* one. The roll freezes when voting starts.
- *Edit deadlines & notes* moves either date.

### After voting closes

1. Open the proposal. Its badge says *Awaiting decision*.
2. Read *Result*: the points per candidate, and the *STAR winner* or a tie.
3. Talk it over with the roll.
4. Click *Appoint* on the chosen candidate, then *Yes, appoint*. Any candidate can be appointed, not only the winner.

Or click *Start new round* to vote again with the same candidates and roll, or *Cancel proposal*.

## What you see

- A badge shows the phase: *Nominating until* a date, *Voting until* a date, *Awaiting decision*, *Appointed* or *Cancelled*.
- Each card of a candidate lists *Current commitments*: the teams they already serve on. You also see their workload badge if you lead one of their teams.
- *Voting members* shows who is on the roll and who has voted, never how they voted.
- The roll is filled in for you: the leader, second and core team members of the team, plus everyone on the team named *Clergy*.
- Each voter gets at most 1 email per night, at 3:30, when they join a roll or when voting opens.
- *Appoint* puts the person on the team in that role at once, exactly as if you had edited the roster.

## If something goes wrong

- *nomination deadline is in the past* or *voting deadline must fall after the nomination deadline*: fix the dates.
- *nominations cannot reopen once ballots are cast*: after the first vote, the nomination date cannot move to today or later.
- *no account — cannot vote* next to a voter: they need a VolunteerDB account. See [Invite a volunteer](invite-a-volunteer.md).
