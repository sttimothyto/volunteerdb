# Change your email address

Move your account to a new email address. The address is how you sign in, and it is the address on every roster you are on.

## Before you start

- You are signed in.
- You can read email at the new address and at the old one.
- The change is staged. Nothing moves until you open the link the site sends to the new address.

## Steps

1. Click the gear icon (*Settings*) at the top right of any page.
2. Click *Password & sign-in*.
3. Find the card *Change your email address*.
4. Type the new address in *New email address*.
5. Click *Send confirmation*.
6. At the new address, open the email *Confirm your new VolunteerDB address*.
7. Click the link in it.
8. On the page *Confirm your new address*, click *Confirm this address*.

## What you see

- After step 5 the page says *Confirmation sent to* and the new address. Nothing changes until the link is opened.
- The card shows *Waiting for* the new address *to confirm*, and the time the link stops working.
- The old address gets an email with the subject *Your VolunteerDB address is being changed*.
- After step 8 the page says *Address confirmed*. Click *Sign in* and sign in with the new address.
- The old address gets a last email, *Your VolunteerDB address changed*. Your rosters now show the new address.
- The *Edit* dialog of your own profile offers the same change. Its *Email* field sends the same link.

## If something goes wrong

- To cancel the change, click *Cancel* on the card. The page says the link no longer works.
- If you do nothing, the link stops working after 24 hours. The account keeps the old address.
- If you typed the wrong address, type the right one and click *Send confirmation* again. The first link dies.
- If the page says *another account already signs in with that address*, ask the parish office.
- If the page says *that is already the address on this account*, there is nothing to change.
- If the page says *Too many address changes requested*, wait 15 minutes. The limit is 5 requests in 15 minutes.
- If the link opens the page *That link did not work*, the link is too old or was used. Ask for the change again.
- If you get the email *Your VolunteerDB address is being changed* and did not ask, click *Cancel* on the card. Then tell the parish office.
