# Send an invite again

Give a volunteer a fresh invitation link when the first one ran out, was lost, or never arrived.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as an administrator. Only an administrator sees the link itself.
- A link works once, and only for a limited time, usually 7 days. After that the row on the *Accounts* page shows *invite expired*.
- An expired link does not lock anyone out. The volunteer can still sign in with an emailed code. See [Sign in with an emailed code](sign-in-with-a-code.md).
- A leader, second or core member can also send a fresh invitation from the team roster. See [Invite a volunteer](invite-a-volunteer.md).

## Steps

Do not use this on an account that already works. The new link removes the password on that account.

1. Click *Accounts* in the header.
2. Find the account in the list.
3. Click the mail icon *New invite link (resets password)* on its row.
4. Read the dialog *Invite link for …*.
5. Click *Copy* if you must give the link to the volunteer yourself.
6. Click *Close*.

## What you see

- The dialog shows the new link and the note *Invite email sent to …*. The site has emailed the link to the address on the account.
- The dialog says how long the link works: usually *Usable once, and only for the next 7 days*.
- The old link stops working the moment the new one exists.
- The row shows *invite pending* until the volunteer opens the link.
- If you click the badge *invite pending*, a dialog says an invitation is out and until when. It cannot show the link: the site keeps only a fingerprint of it.

## If something goes wrong

- If the dialog says the invite email could not be sent, then give the volunteer the copied link yourself. Hand it over in person, on paper, or from your own mailbox.
- If the address on the account is wrong, then switch the account off and unlink it from the volunteer. Then create a new account at the right address. See [Manage accounts](manage-accounts.md).
