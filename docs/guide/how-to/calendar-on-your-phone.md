# Put the parish calendar on your phone

Subscribe the calendar on your phone to the events of the parish, or to your own duties. The calendar then stays up to date by itself.

## Before you start

- You are signed in.
- There are 2 feeds. The parish feed holds the events of every team. Your personal feed holds the slots you signed up for.
- A subscription refreshes itself about once an hour. A downloaded file is a copy, made once, and never changes.
- For Apple Calendar, do the steps on the phone itself. For Google Calendar, do them on a computer; the phone follows.

## Steps

1. Click *Events* in the header.
2. Above the month calendar, click *Whole parish* for the parish feed, or *My duties* for your duties.
3. Click *Add to your calendar*, at the right of the 2 views.
4. On an iPhone or iPad, click *Subscribe in Apple Calendar, Outlook or Thunderbird*. Then confirm in the Calendar app.
5. For the parish feed in Google Calendar, click *Add to Google Calendar*. Then confirm in Google.
6. For your duties in Google Calendar, copy the *Feed address* in the panel.
7. In Google Calendar on the computer, open *Other calendars → + → From URL*. Paste the address.
8. Click *Close*.

## What you see

- The title of the panel for the parish feed is *The whole parish, in your own calendar*.
- The title of the panel for your duties is *Your duties, in your own calendar*.
- A new calendar appears in your calendar app. The name of the parish feed is the name of the parish, followed by *events*. The name of your duties is *My duties*.
- A calendar added to Google Calendar on the computer appears in the Google Calendar app on the phone too.
- Every slot you sign up for appears in your duties. Every slot you leave disappears.
- The panel for your duties is also on the page *Your account*: the gear icon (*Settings*), then *Password & sign-in*.

## If something goes wrong

- If *Add to Google Calendar* is not in the panel, the parish has no Google calendar. Use the *Feed address* line instead.
- The address of your personal feed is private to you. Anyone who has it can read your duties.
- If the address gets out, click *Reset the address* in the panel. The old address no longer works. Subscribe again with the new one.
- If you want a copy only, click *Download a .ics file*. Open the file in your calendar app. It does not update.
- If the calendar on the phone is behind, wait an hour. It refreshes itself.
