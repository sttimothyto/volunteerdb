# Add or change your photo

Put a picture of your face on your record. It shows in the header, on your profile, and in the side panel your teammates open.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and your account is linked to a volunteer record.
- You have a picture of your face on the device you use. Any common picture format works, up to 10 MB.
- The site crops the picture to a small square, 400 × 400. Choose a picture with your face near the middle.
- You must confirm that the photo is appropriate and professional. The declaration says that illegal or explicit content will be reported to the authorities.

## Steps

1. Click the person icon, or your current photo, at the right end of the header.
2. Read the title of the dialog. It says *Photo —* followed by your name.
3. Click the box *Drop a headshot here (stored as 400×400 JPEG)* and pick your picture.
4. Look at the preview. It shows the picture as the site will store it.
5. Tick the checkbox that starts with *I confirm this is an appropriate professional photo*.
6. Click *Upload*.

## What you see

- The tooltip of the icon in the header says *Add or change photo*.
- After step 6 the page says *Photo saved*, and it loads again with the new photo.
- The same dialog opens from your profile page. Click the icon or the photo beside your name at the top of the card.
- On a phone the header hides your address, but the icon or photo stays. It is the mark of who is signed in.

## If something goes wrong

- If *Upload* stays grey, you have not ticked the checkbox.
- If the page says *Choose a photo first*, you picked no picture. Do step 3 again.
- If the page says *image file larger than 10 MB*, pick a smaller picture.
- If the page says *not a readable image*, the file is not a picture. Pick a JPEG or PNG file.
- If the page says *image does not compress to a storable size*, pick a simpler picture.
- To take your photo away, open the dialog and click *Remove photo*. The page says *Photo removed*.
- To leave without a change, click *Cancel*.
