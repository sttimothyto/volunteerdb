# Use dark mode

Show the site as light text on a dark background.

## Before you start

- You are signed in.
- The choice is kept on this device, in this browser. It is not part of your account.

## Steps

1. Click the gear icon (*Settings*) at the top right of any page.
2. Click the switch *Dark mode*.

## What you see

- The page turns dark at once.
- Every page of the site, and the sign-in page, uses the same choice on this device.
- The choice stays after you sign out. Another device, or another browser, has its own choice.
- To go back, click the gear icon and click *Dark mode* again.
