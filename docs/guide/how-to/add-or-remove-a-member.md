# Add or remove a member

Put a volunteer on the roster of your team, or take one off it.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of the team, or of a team above it.
- You look at today, not at a past date. The roster controls are hidden on an as-of view.
- The person is already in the parish list. A leader cannot create a new person in the app; see the note below.

## Steps

### Add a volunteer from the parish list

1. Open *Teams* and click the team.
2. Under *Add member*, click *Volunteer* and type part of the name.
3. Click the name in the list.
4. Click *Role* and choose a role. The default is *Member*.
5. Click *Add*.

You can also start from the profile page of the person. Under *Add to team*, pick the *Team* and the *Role*, then click *Add*.

### Add a person who is not in the parish list yet

- Only an administrator sees *New volunteer* on the *Volunteers* page. Ask them to add the person, then add the person to the team as above.
- Or bring the person in with a spreadsheet, on a row that names one of your teams and a role. See [Import a roster from a .csv file](import-a-csv.md) and [Link a roster spreadsheet](link-a-roster-spreadsheet.md).

### Remove a member

1. On the team page, find the person under *Roster*.
2. Click the icon of a person with a minus sign, at the right end of the row. Its tooltip says *Remove from team*.

## What you see

- After *Add*, the page reloads and the person appears under *Roster*, in role order.
- If the person was already on the team, their role changes to the one you chose instead.
- After a removal, the row is gone at once. There is no confirmation step.
- The person stays in the parish list and on their other teams. Their sign-in account is not touched.
- The site keeps the membership as history. An as-of view of an earlier date still shows the person on the team.
- Neither action sends an email.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says *Pick a volunteer*, no name was chosen in *Volunteer*.
- If the name is not in the *Volunteer* list, the person is not in the parish list yet. See above.
- If you see no *Add member* section, you do not manage this team, or you are on an as-of view.
