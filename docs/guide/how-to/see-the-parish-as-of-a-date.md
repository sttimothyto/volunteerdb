# See the parish as it was on a past date

Open the *Dashboard*, the *Teams* page or a team page as it was at the end of a past day.

## Before you start

- You are signed in.
- The site keeps every change. A past date shows the teams, the rosters and the roles as they were then.
- Only the *Dashboard*, the *Teams* page and the team pages can show a past date. The other pages show today only.

## Steps

1. Open the *Dashboard*, the *Teams* page or a team page.
2. Click the gear icon (*Settings*) at the top right of the page.
3. Find the box *View as of (YYYY-MM-DD)* at the bottom of the menu.
4. Type the date, or click the calendar button (*Choose a date*) and pick it.
5. Click *View*.

## What you see

- A yellow banner at the top of the page says *Read-only snapshot as of* followed by the date and the time.
- The gear icon turns yellow while you read a snapshot.
- A date means the end of that day. The page includes every change made on that day.
- The team pages, the roster and the side panel of a volunteer follow the snapshot. Links between them keep the date.
- On the *Dashboard*, the note under *Parish* says that shifts, elections and sign-ins are left out.
- You cannot change anything on a snapshot. The buttons that edit are not on the page.

## If something goes wrong

- To come back to today, click *Back to now* in the banner.
- You can also clear the box in the gear menu and click *View*.
- If you type a date that is not real, the page shows today, without a banner.
- If the volunteer record did not exist at that date, the side panel says *No volunteer with id* followed by a number.
- Photos and sign-in details are not kept in the history. A snapshot shows the photo and the *Last login* of today.
