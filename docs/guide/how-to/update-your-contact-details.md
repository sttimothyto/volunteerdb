# Update your contact details

Change the name, the phone number or the notes on your own volunteer record. Your teams see the change on their rosters at once.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and your account is linked to a volunteer record. If your email address in the header does not open a page, ask the parish office.
- Your email address is also how you sign in, so it changes by a different route. See [Change your email address](change-your-email-address.md).

## Steps

1. Click your email address at the right end of the header.
2. Click *Edit* at the top right of the card.
3. Check *First name* and *Last name*, and correct them if needed.
4. Type your phone number in *Phone*.
5. Type what your leaders must know in *Notes*, for example the days you cannot serve.
6. Fill in the other fields of the dialog, if the administrator added any.
7. Click *Save*.

## What you see

- The title of the dialog is *Edit* followed by your name.
- After step 7 the page loads again. The card shows the new *Phone* and *Notes*.
- The leaders, the seconds and the core members of your teams see the new phone number on the roster.
- *Notes* are for you, the leaders and seconds of your teams, and the administrators. Core members do not see them.
- A date field has a calendar button, *Choose a date*. You can also type the date as YYYY-MM-DD.
- On a phone the header hides your address. Open a team page, click your own name, then click *Full profile*.

## If something goes wrong

- If you empty *Email* and click *Save*, the page says *Your own address cannot be blank — it is how you sign in.* Type it again.
- If you type a new address in *Email*, the site does not save it at once. It emails a link to the new address first. See [Change your email address](change-your-email-address.md).
- To leave without a change, click *Cancel*.
