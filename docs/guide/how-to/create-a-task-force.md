# Create a task force

Staff 1 event with the members of 2 or more teams.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of the team of the event.
- The event exists and has not ended. See [Create an event with shifts](create-an-event.md).
- Ask the leader of the other team first. The site does not ask them for you.

## Steps

1. Open *Events* and click the event.
2. Under *Collaboration*, click *Add collaborating team* and pick the team.
3. Click *Add*.
4. Read the question, then click *Add team*.

Repeat the steps for each further team.

## What you see

- The message *Team added — their roster can sign up now*.
- The *Collaboration* card gets a *task force* badge and a line *Staffed by:* with the teams.
- The site made a temporary team for the event. It is a sub-team of your team, named after the event and its date, with *task force* at the end.
- It appears on *Teams* with a description that starts with *[Auto]*.
- Every member of every team that staffs the event is on it, with the highest role they hold on any of those teams.
- The members of the added team see the event on *Events* and can sign up.
- The leaders and seconds of the added team manage the event with you: slots, assignments, cancellation.
- *Sync rosters* copies in the people who joined one of those teams since. Nothing is copied in the background.
- After the event ends, or if it is cancelled, the site removes the temporary team within 1 hour. The event goes back to your team. Sign-ups and attendance are kept.
- The temporary team stays in history. An as-of view of the date of the event still shows it.

## What a task force cannot see

- A task force gives rights over the event, never over the people. Contact details, notes, workload, invites and exports of the members of the other team stay with their own team.
- On the team page of the task force, everybody sees names only, with no contact details.
- The nightly sync never makes a roster spreadsheet for a task force.
- Even an administrator cannot delete the temporary team while the event is on.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says *that team already staffs this event*, it is on the task force already.
- If it says *cannot add a collaborating team: event has already ended* or *event is cancelled*, the event is closed.
- If a team is not in the *Add collaborating team* list, it is inactive, or it already staffs the event.
