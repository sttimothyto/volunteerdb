# Manage accounts

Create an account for a volunteer, make someone an administrator, switch an account off or on, and link an account to a volunteer.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as an administrator.
- Click *Accounts* in the header. The *Accounts* page lists every account, 1 row each.
- A leader, second or core member can give a member of their own team an account from the roster. See [Invite a volunteer](invite-a-volunteer.md).

## Steps

### Create an account for a volunteer

1. Click *New account*.
2. Type the volunteer's email address in *Email (login)*.
3. Leave *Linked volunteer* on *— match by email —*, or pick the volunteer from the list.
4. Leave *Parish admin (full access)* off.
5. Click *Create*.

The site emails the volunteer an invitation. The dialog *Invite link for …* also shows the link, and *Copy* copies it. See [Send an invite again](resend-an-invite.md) for what to do with the link.

### Make someone an administrator

1. Find the account in the list.
2. Click the key icon *Make admin* on its row.

To take the right away, click the key icon *Revoke admin* on the same row.

### Switch an account off or on

1. Find the account in the list.
2. Click the icon *Disable* on its row.

The row shows the badge *disabled*, and the person can no longer sign in. Their volunteer record and their teams stay as they are. Click *Enable* on the row to switch the account on again.

### Link an account to a volunteer

1. Find the account in the list. The small grey line under the address names the linked volunteer, or says *not linked to a volunteer*.
2. Click the link icon *Change linked volunteer*.
3. Pick the volunteer in the list, or pick *— not linked —*.
4. Click *Save*.

### Create accounts for everyone at once

1. Click *Create accounts for all volunteers with email*.
2. Read the dialog, then click *Create and email invites*.

Every active volunteer with an email address and no account gets an account and an invitation email. The page then says how many accounts it created, linked and skipped.

## What you see

- Each row shows the email address, the linked volunteer and a badge for the sign-in state. Once the person has signed in, the row also shows *last login* with the date.
- The badge *invite pending* means the invitation link is out and still works. *invite expired* means it ran out.
- The badge *email-code sign-in* means the person has no password and signs in with an emailed code.
- The icon at the left of a row is a shield for an administrator, and a grey person for everyone else.
- After you change a link, the page shows the address, an arrow, and the volunteer's name.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says *that volunteer is already linked to …*, then that volunteer already has an account. Unlink that account first, or leave the link as it is.
- If the dialog says the invite email could not be sent, then click *Copy* and give the link to the volunteer yourself.
- If the page says *Admins only.*, then your account is not an administrator account.
