# Read the audit log

Find out who changed what, and when, from the record the site keeps of its own activity.

## Before you start

- The audit log is not a page in the app. It is kept on the computer that runs the site, and the person who runs the site reads it.
- Every page can also show the past. See [See the parish as of a date](see-the-parish-as-of-a-date.md). That answers most questions about what a record looked like before, without the log.

## Steps

1. Write down what you want to know: which record, which person, and roughly when.
2. Ask the person who runs the site to look in the audit log.
3. Read the answer with the lists below in mind.

## What is recorded

- Every change that is saved: a new record, an edit with the old and the new value, and a deletion. Each line names the account that made the change and the time.
- Every sign-in, every sign-in code requested, and every failed attempt to sign in.
- Every invitation created or accepted, every password set or removed, and every change of email address.
- Every time a leader, a second or an administrator changes another person's email address.
- Every roster export, with who did it and for which teams.
- Every `.csv` import, with who did it and how many records it changed.
- Every hand-over of a shift, and every time a person takes themselves off a shift, with their reason.
- Every substitute request that hit the team's daily email limit.
- Every roster spreadsheet created or synced, and every collaborating team added to an event.

## What is not recorded

- The pages a person looked at. At the usual setting, reading is not written to the log.
- Passwords, sign-in codes, invitation links, address-change links, personal calendar feed addresses, and ballot scores. The log shows *«redacted»* in their place.
- The full text of a very long note. The log cuts it short at 120 characters.
