# Create an event with shifts

Put an event on the calendar of your team, with the slots the members sign up for.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of a team, or of a team above it.
- You know the date, the start and end times, the place, and the slots you need: for example 2 lectors and 4 greeters.
- The title, time, place and description can go on the public parish calendar. Put nothing private in them.

## Steps

1. Open *Events*.
2. Click *New event*, at the top right of *Upcoming events on your teams*.
3. Click *Team* and pick the team. The list holds only the teams you lead.
4. Type the name of the event in *Title*.
5. Type the day in *Date (YYYY-MM-DD)*, or click the calendar icon and pick it.
6. Type the times in *Starts (HH:MM)* and *Ends (HH:MM)*, on a 24-hour clock.
7. Type the place in *Location (optional)* and any notes in *Description (optional)*.
8. Under *Slots*, type the name of the first slot in *Slot*. It says *Volunteers* to start with.
9. Type how many people the slot takes in *Capacity*. Leave it blank for no limit.
10. Click *Add another slot* for each further slot, and fill in its row.
11. For a weekly event, type the last date in *Repeat weekly until (YYYY-MM-DD, optional)*.
12. Click *Create event*.
13. If the dialog *Possible double booking* opens, read it. Click *Go back* or *Create anyway*.

## What you see

- The page of the new event: the team, a blue badge with the date, the time and the place. Below is 1 card per slot, with a badge such as *0/2*.
- A slot row with no name is ignored. If every row is blank, the event gets 1 slot named *Volunteers* with no limit.
- With a repeat, the message *N events created*, and the page of the first week. Each week is a separate event with its own slots; edit or cancel each one on its own.
- A repeat is limited to 1 year. The clock time stays the same across the change to summer time.
- The members of the team see the event on *Events* and on the team page under *Upcoming events*. They can sign up for a slot.
- *Possible double booking* lists events of any team at the same place on the same day. An event you cannot see shows as *Another team's event*.
- If the parish has a public Google calendar, the event appears on it within 30 minutes. The calendar shows the title, time, place and description, never the slots or the names.
- *Edit* on the page of the event changes the *Title*, *Date (YYYY-MM-DD)*, *Starts (HH:MM)*, *Ends (HH:MM)*, *Location* and *Description*.

## Add a description to a slot

The *New event* dialog takes a name and a capacity for each slot. To add a note such as *main door, from 10:00*:

1. On the page of the event, click the pencil icon on the slot card. Its tooltip starts with *Rename this slot*.
2. Type the note in the box whose label starts with *Description (optional)*. It holds at most 300 characters.
3. Click *Save*.

*Add slot*, under the slots, adds a slot with a *Slot name*, a *Capacity (blank = unlimited)* and a *Description (optional)*. The bin icon removes a slot that has nobody on it.

## Reminders

- A person you put on a slot with *Assign* gets the nightly email *VolunteerDB: your upcoming service*, sent at 4:00. It also carries the reminder the day before.
- A person who signs up chooses the reminders in the *Sign up* dialog. *7 days before* is off unless ticked; *24 hours before* is on unless unticked.
- Nobody gets more than 1 of these emails a night, whatever the number of events.

## If something goes wrong

- *Pick the team*: nothing was chosen in *Team*.
- *a title is required*: fill in *Title*.
- *Start: use YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM*, or the same for *End*: the date or a time is not in that shape.
- *the event must end after it starts*: check the 2 times.
- *slot names must be unique within the event*: 2 slots have the same name.
- *slot capacity must be at least 1 (or blank for unlimited)*: type 1 or more, or clear the box.
- *the repeat-until date is before the first event* or *repeats are limited to one year of events*: fix the repeat date.
- If you see no *New event* button, you lead no team, or the team is inactive.
