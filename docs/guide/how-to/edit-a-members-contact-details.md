# Edit the contact details of a member

Correct the name, email address, phone number or notes of a volunteer on your team.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of one of the teams of the person, or of a team above it.
- A task force does not count. To edit a member borrowed for an event, you must lead one of their own teams.

## Steps

1. Open *Teams* and click the team.
2. Under *Roster*, click the name of the person. A panel slides in.
3. Click *Full profile* at the bottom of the panel.
4. Click *Edit*, at the top right of the card.
5. Change *First name*, *Last name*, *Email*, *Phone* or *Notes*.
6. Fill in any custom field of the parish, below *Notes*.
7. Click *Save*.

You can also start from *Volunteers*: click the row of the person, then *Full profile*.

## What you can change

- *First name* and *Last name*.
- *Email*: the address on the roster and in exports, and the address that event and election emails go to. An invite link goes there too.
- *Phone*.
- *Notes*: shown only to leaders, seconds and administrators. A core team member does not see them.
- The custom fields the administrator added, for example a date or a yes/no switch.
- A blank box clears the value. A spreadsheet import is different: there, a blank cell changes nothing.

## What you cannot change

- The *Active* switch. Only an administrator archives or deletes a volunteer.
- The teams of the person. Use *Add to team* on the same page, or the *Roster* of the team.
- The sign-in address or the password of their account. Only the person changes those.
- The photo is not in the dialog. Click the picture next to the name instead; anyone who is signed in can change a photo.

## What you see

- The dialog closes and the page reloads. The card shows the new *Email:*, *Phone:* and *Notes:* lines. There is no message.
- The new details are on the roster of every team of the person, and in the next export.
- If you changed the email address of somebody else, the change is immediate. The old address gets the email *Your VolunteerDB address was changed*, which says who to tell if the change is news to them.
- If the person already has an account, they still sign in with the address they had. See [Change your email address](change-your-email-address.md).
- Your own address goes another way. The dialog says *Changing your own address sends a confirmation link to the new one; nothing moves until you open it.*
- The old details are kept as history. An as-of view of an earlier date still shows them.

## If something goes wrong

- If you see no *Edit* button, you do not lead one of the teams of the person. A task force does not count.
- If the page says *Your own address cannot be blank — it is how you sign in.*, type your address back in.
- If the page says *Too many address changes requested — try again in a few minutes.*, wait 15 minutes. This limit is for your own address only.
- If a custom field refuses the value, read the message. It says the shape the field expects.
