# Read the full roster of your team

Read the email addresses and the phone numbers of the members of your team, and reach them.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and you are a core member, the second or the leader of the team.
- A plain member sees the names, the roles and who can sign in. You also see the contact details.
- The same applies to the sub-teams of your team. A task force shows names only.
- Contact details are personal information. Use them for the ministry only.

## Steps

1. Click *Teams* in the header.
2. Click your team in the list.
3. Find the section *Roster*.
4. Read each row: the name, the role, the email address, the phone number, and the account badge.
5. Click a name to open the side panel with the details of that person.
6. Click *Full profile* in the panel to open the whole profile page.

## What you see

- The side panel shows *Email*, *Phone*, the extra fields of the parish, *Last login* and *Serves on*.
- The profile page shows the same, plus *Service hours* and the section *If they leave, what vacancies appear?*
- Above the roster are the buttons *Export roster (.csv)*, *Copy email list* and *Email all (BCC)*.
- *Copy email list* copies every address on the roster. The page says how many addresses it copied.
- *Email all (BCC)* opens your mail app with every address in BCC. For a very large team, use *Copy email list*.
- The section *Volunteer home page* is on the page for you too.
- On the *Volunteers* page, the columns *Email* and *Phone* show the details of the people on your teams. For everyone else they show *•••*.
- A row with the badge *no account* offers to invite that person. See [Invite a volunteer to create an account](invite-a-volunteer.md).

## If something goes wrong

- If the roster shows names only, you are a plain member of this team. Ask the leader for the core role.
- If the page says that the roster is not visible to you, you are not on this team at all. The message starts with *You are not on this team*.
- *Notes* and the workload of a member are for the leader and the second only.
- If you read a past date, the buttons to copy and email are not on the page. Click *Back to now*.
