# Vote in an election

Score the candidates for a leadership seat on a team, while the vote is open.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and your name is on the voting roll of the proposal. A leader of the team puts you there.
- The site emails you *VolunteerDB elections: your input is needed* the night after you join a roll, and the night after the vote opens.
- The vote is open until the end of the day *Voting closes*, in the time zone of the parish. You can change your ballot until then.
- Your scores are secret. The site shows who has voted, and the totals after the vote closes.

## Steps

1. Click *Dash* in the header.
2. Under *My service*, click the tile *Ballots waiting*. Or click *Elections* in the header.
3. Under *Open proposals*, click the proposal. Its badge says *Voting until* followed by the date.
4. Read the cards under *Candidates*. Each shows the note of the nominator and the *Current commitments*.
5. Find the section *Your ballot*.
6. For each candidate, click a score from 0 (worst) to 5 (best).
7. Click *Submit ballot*.

## What you see

- The page says *Ballot recorded*, and the date until which you can change it.
- Under *Voting members*, a ballot icon appears next to your name. The line above counts the ballots cast.
- Score every candidate on their own merits. Two similar candidates do not split your vote.
- A candidate you leave at 0 counts as 0. There is no abstention.
- To change your ballot, open the proposal again, move the scores and click *Submit ballot*.
- After the day *Voting closes*, the section *Result* appears. It shows the points of each candidate and the winner, or a tie.
- The result is advice. The leaders debate, then appoint a candidate, or start a new round.

## If something goes wrong

- If *Ballots waiting* is not on the *Dashboard*, no open vote waits for you.
- If the page says *cannot vote: proposal is* followed by a phase, the vote is closed, or not yet open. The badge shows the phase.
- If a name on the roll says *no account — cannot vote*, that person cannot sign in. A leader or a core member can invite them.
- While nominations are open, you can add a candidate: pick a name under *New candidate*, type *Why them?*, then click *Nominate*.
