# Change your password

Set a new password for your own account, or add one if you sign in with emailed codes.

## Before you start

- You are signed in.
- If you signed in with an emailed code, you do not need your old password.
- If you forgot your password, sign out and sign in with an emailed code. Then come back here.
- A password must have 15 characters or more. Spaces and any other characters are fine. Capitals, digits and symbols are not required.
- The easiest password is a phrase of 4 or 5 unrelated words, for example *green kettle Tuesday harbour*.
- The site refuses well-known passwords, and light disguises of them, for example *Password2026* or *letmein!!*.
- It also refuses your own email address, the name of the site or the parish, a repeated pattern, and a run along the keyboard.

## Steps

1. Click the gear icon (*Settings*) at the top right of any page.
2. Click *Password & sign-in*.
3. Find the card *Change your password* (or *Set a password*).
4. If the card shows *Current password*, type your old password in it.
5. Type the new password in *New password*.
6. Type it again in *Repeat new password*.
7. Click *Save password*.

## What you see

- The page says *Password saved. You can sign in with it from now on.*
- The site sends an email with the subject *Your VolunteerDB password changed* to your address.
- If you did not make the change, tell the parish office straight away.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says *That password is too short*, use 15 characters or more.
- If the page says *That password is a well-known one*, choose a different phrase.
- If the page says *That password is your email address or the name of this site*, choose something unrelated to the account.
- If the page says *That is not your current password*, type the old password again. After 5 wrong tries in 15 minutes, wait.
- If the two new passwords are not the same, the page says so. Type them again.
- To stop the use of a password, click *Remove password* on the same card. You then sign in with emailed codes.
