# Report a problem

Tell the right person about content that is wrong or inappropriate, or about a page that does not work.

## Before you start

- The site has no report button. You report by email, or by a word with a person.
- A wrong name, phone number or role on a roster: tell the leader or the second of that team. They can correct it.
- A mistake on your own record: correct it yourself. See [Update your contact details](update-your-contact-details.md).
- A page that does not work, or an account you cannot open: ask the administrator of the site. The parish office knows who that is.
- Inappropriate content, for example a photo, a note or a team home page: send it to the address in the steps below.

## Steps

1. Note the page you were on, and what you saw. The name of the page is at the top.
2. Click the gear icon (*Settings*) at the top right of any page, then click *Manual*.
3. Scroll to the bottom of any page of the manual.
4. Find the line *See something inappropriate? Report it to* followed by an email address.
5. Click the address. Your mail app opens a new message.
6. Write the page and what you saw, then send the message.

## What you see

- The address is chosen by the parish, and a person reads that mailbox.
- The report is not shown on the site, and the site does not confirm it. The person who reads the mailbox answers you.
- Everyone who uploads a photo declares that it is appropriate and professional. The declaration says that illegal or explicit content will be reported to the authorities.
- If you get an email from the site that you did not expect, tell the parish office straight away. An example is *Your VolunteerDB address was changed*.
