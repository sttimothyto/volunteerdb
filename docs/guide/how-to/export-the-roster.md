# Export the roster

Download the roster of your team as a `.csv` file, which opens in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader, second-in-command or core team member of the team.

## Steps

### One team

1. Open *Teams* and click the team.
2. Click *Export roster (.csv)*.

The file is named after the team, for example `music-ministry.csv`. It holds the team and its sub-teams.

### Every team you help run

1. Open *Teams*.
2. Click *Export team(s)*, at the top right.

The file is named `volunteerdb-my-teams.csv`. It holds every team where you are a leader, a second or a core team member, with their sub-teams. An administrator gets the whole parish instead, as `volunteerdb-parish.csv`.

## What you see

- Your browser saves the file. Nothing changes on the site.
- One row per person per team, with the 8 roster columns, then 1 column per custom field. See [Import a roster from a .csv file](import-a-csv.md) for the columns.
- *Volunteer notes* is filled in only if you lead or second every team in the file. A core team member gets the column blank.
- Photos are not in the file.
- On an as-of view, *Export roster (.csv)* gives the roster as it was on that date.
- Every export is written to the audit log.

## Two buttons next to *Export roster (.csv)*

- *Copy email list* copies the address of everyone on the roster. The page says how many addresses were copied.
- *Email all (BCC)* opens your mail program with everyone in BCC. For a very large team, use *Copy email list* instead.

## If something goes wrong

- If you see no *Export roster (.csv)* button, you are a member of the team, not a core team member or a leader.
- If you see no *Export team(s)* button on *Teams*, you hold none of those 3 roles on any team.
