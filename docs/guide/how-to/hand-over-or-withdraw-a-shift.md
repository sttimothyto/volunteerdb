# Hand over or withdraw a shift

Put a member on a slot, take one off, or pass your own shift to a teammate, and know who is emailed.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of the team of the event, or of a team above it.
- The event has not ended and is not cancelled. After the end, the names on the slots are frozen; only *Attendance* can change.
- For a task force, the leaders and seconds of every team that staffs the event can do all of this.

## Schedule a member

1. Open *Events* and click the event.
2. Under *Slots*, find the slot. Its badge, for example *1/2*, counts the people on it.
3. Click *Schedule someone* and pick the name. People who answered *Available* come first, marked *· available*.
4. Click *Assign*.

The name appears on the slot at once. The person gets the nightly email *VolunteerDB: your upcoming service* at 4:00, under *You have been scheduled to serve:*, and a reminder the day before. A name marked *· UNAVAILABLE* answered *Not available*. You can still assign them; their row then shows the badge *marked unavailable*.

## Take a member off a slot

1. On the row of the person, click *Remove*.

The row is gone at once. There is no question, no reason to give, and nobody is emailed. Tell the person yourself. To move a shift from one member to another, click *Remove*, then *Assign* the other.

## Hand over your own shift

Do this only after the teammate agreed to take the shift.

1. On your own row, click *Hand off*.
2. Read the dialog *Hand this slot to a teammate*.
3. Click *Who takes it?* and pick the teammate.
4. Click *Hand it over*.

The message says the name of the teammate, then *now holds the slot*. The teammate gets the email *You're now serving:* with the title of the event, at once. Their row shows the badge *substitute*. If you had asked for a substitute, that request is closed. The hand-over goes into the log, with who did it and when.

## Withdraw from your own shift

1. On your own row, click *Withdraw*.
2. Read the dialog *Take yourself off this slot*.
3. Type your reason in *Why can you no longer serve?* A reason is required.
4. Click *Take me off*.

The message *You're off the slot — the leaders have been told* appears. Every leader and second of the team gets the email *Off the roster:* with the title of the event and your reason. The slot is open again.

## Which button is which

- *Need a sub* asks the whole team by email, and the first to claim the slot takes it. See [Ask for a substitute](ask-for-a-substitute.md).
- *Withdraw request*, on *Events* under *Your upcoming duties*, takes back that call. It does not take you off the slot.
- *Hand off* and *Withdraw* are on your own row only. On the row of another member, a leader sees *Remove*.

## If something goes wrong

- *Pick a person first* or *Pick a teammate first*: nothing was chosen in the list.
- *they already serve at this event*: a person holds 1 slot per event. Remove them from the other slot first.
- A slot name followed by *is full*: raise the capacity with the pencil icon on the slot, or pick another slot.
- *only members of the event's team can take part*: the person is not on the roster of the team of the event.
- *cannot join this event: event has already ended*, or *event is cancelled*: the event is closed.
- If *Schedule someone* is not on a slot, the slot is full, or everybody on the roster is already at the event.
