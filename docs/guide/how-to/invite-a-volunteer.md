# Invite a volunteer to create an account

Email a member of your team a link to set up their own account on the site.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and you are a core member, the second or the leader of a team the volunteer is on.
- The volunteer has an email address on their record. Without one, you cannot invite them.
- The volunteer has no account yet, or an account nobody has used. The roster badge says *no account* or *invite expired*.
- The link works once, and for 7 days. After that, the volunteer can still sign in with an emailed code.
- The site emails the link to the address on the record. You never see the link yourself; only an administrator does.

## Steps

1. Click *Teams* in the header, then click your team.
2. In the *Roster*, find the row of the volunteer. It shows the badge *no account*.
3. Point at the badge. It changes to *invite to create account*. Click it.
4. Read the dialog. It says *Send an invite to* followed by the name.
5. Click *Send an invite*.
6. Read the dialog *Invite link for* followed by the address. It says *Invite email sent to* and the address.
7. Click *Close*.

## What you see

- The page says *Invite emailed to* followed by the address.
- The row now shows the badge *invite sent*. Its tooltip says until when the link works.
- The volunteer gets an email, *Your VolunteerDB account*, followed by the name of the parish. The link in it opens the page *Finish your account setup*.
- A password on that page is optional. The volunteer also agrees to keep personal information confidential.
- After they finish, the row shows *account* and the date they last signed in. The volunteer gets a second email, *Your VolunteerDB account is ready*.
- The same control is on the profile page of the volunteer, next to *Last login*, and in the side panel.

## If something goes wrong

- If the badge stays *no account* and its tooltip says *No email address on file*, add an address first. A leader or a second can. See [Edit the contact details of a member](edit-a-members-contact-details.md).
- If the row shows *invite sent*, an invite is already out. Click the badge, then *Send again* to send a fresh link. The old link no longer works.
- If the row shows *invite expired*, point at it and click *send a new invite*.
- If the page says that the invite email could not be sent, check the address, then send again. Or ask an administrator for the link.
- If the row shows *disabled*, the account is switched off. Only an administrator can turn it on again.
- You cannot invite anyone from a past date. Click *Back to now* first.
