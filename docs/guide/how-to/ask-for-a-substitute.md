# Ask for a substitute

Ask your teammates to take over a slot you hold at an upcoming event.

## Before you start

- You are signed in, and you hold a slot at an event that has not ended.
- The site emails every member of the team who has an email address and is not already at the event. The first to claim the slot takes it.
- You stay on the slot until a teammate takes it. If nobody does, you still serve.
- A team can send 6 of these calls in 24 hours. After that, the site posts a request but emails nobody.
- If you already know who will take the slot, use *Hand off* instead. See [Hand over or withdraw a shift](hand-over-or-withdraw-a-shift.md).

## Steps

1. Click *Events* in the header.
2. Under *Your upcoming duties*, find the event. Or open the event page and find your name under *Slots*.
3. Click *Need a sub* next to the event, or next to your name.
4. Read the dialog *Ask for a substitute*.
5. Type a short note in *Note to the team (optional)*, for example why, or until when.
6. Click *Ask the team*.

## What you see

- The page says *Asked* followed by the number of teammates emailed.
- The badge *sub wanted* appears next to your name on the event, and next to the event under *Your upcoming duties*.
- Your teammates get an email, *Substitute needed:* followed by the name of the event. Your note is in it.
- The request also appears on the *Events* page of every teammate, under *Teammates need a substitute*.
- When a teammate takes the slot, you get an email, *Substitute found:* followed by the name of the event. Your name leaves the slot.
- To take the request back, click *Withdraw request* next to the event under *Your upcoming duties*. The page says *Request withdrawn*.

## If something goes wrong

- If the page says that the team has already sent its 6 substitute emails for today, the site emailed nobody. Your request is still on the *Events* page. Ask a teammate directly, or try again tomorrow.
- If the page says *a substitute request is already open for this slot*, your earlier request is still open.
- If the page says *cannot ask for a substitute: event has already ended*, the event is past. Tell your leader instead.
- If the page says *cannot ask for a substitute: event is cancelled*, there is nothing to do.
- A note is cut after 200 characters.
