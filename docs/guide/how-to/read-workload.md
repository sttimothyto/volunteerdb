# Read the workload of your people

See how much each volunteer on your team already carries across the whole parish, before you ask for more.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as a leader or second-in-command of one of the teams of the person, or of a team above it.
- Core team members do not see workload, and nobody sees their own.
- A task force does not count as one of the teams of the person.

## Steps

1. Open *Teams* and click the team.
2. Under *Roster*, click the name of the person.
3. In the panel that slides in, find the coloured badge under the name. It reads *workload:*, then the band and the score.

## What you see

- A badge such as *workload: amber · 4.5*. The same badge is on the profile page of the person.
- On *Volunteers*, the *Workload* column shows the badge for every person you lead. The *Workload* box next to the search field filters the list to 1 band.
- On the *Dashboard*, the line *Workload:* counts the people on your teams in each band. Click a band to open that list.
- In the ministry graph on the *Dashboard*, the dot of a volunteer takes the colour of their band.
- On a proposal page under *Elections*, the card of each candidate shows their band.
- A person with no badge is on none of your teams, or is you.

## What a band means

The score adds up every team the person is on: the weight of the team, times a factor for the role. With the standard settings:

| Role | Factor |
|---|---|
| *Ministry leader* | 3 |
| *Second-in-command* | 2 |
| *Core team member* | 1.5 |
| *Member* | 1 |

| Band | Score |
|---|---|
| green | up to 4 |
| amber | more than 4, up to 8 |
| red | more than 8 |

The weight of a team says how demanding it is. A new team starts at 1; a team with weight 0 counts for nothing.

Example: a person leads a team of weight 3 and is a member of a team of weight 1. The score is 3 × 3 + 1 × 1 = 10, which is red.

- The score is parish-wide. It counts teams you have nothing to do with. That is the point: you see what the person already carries elsewhere.
- An administrator can change the factors, the bands and the weights. The settings of your parish can differ from the tables above.
- On an as-of view, the score uses the memberships of that date, but the factors and bands of today.
