# Your teams and your service

In this tutorial you find your teams, read a roster, and complete your own profile with your contact details and a photo. It takes about 15 minutes.

## What you need

- You are signed in. If not, follow [Sign in for the first time](first-sign-in.md).
- You are on at least one team. If not, ask your team leader to add you.
- A picture of your face, saved on the device you use, if you want to add a photo.

## 1. Find your teams on the Dashboard

1. Click *Dash* in the header.
2. Find the section *My teams*.
3. Read the badge next to each team name. It is your role on that team.
4. Click the name of one of your teams.

You see the page of that team. Its title is the name of the team. A sub-team shows the name of its parent team first. The 4 roles are *Member*, *Core team member*, *Second-in-command* and *Ministry leader*.

## 2. Read the roster

1. Read the description under the title, if the team has one.
2. Find the section *Roster*.
3. Read one row. It shows a name, a role badge, and a badge that says *account* or *no account*.
4. Find the section *Sub-teams*, if the team has sub-teams. Each sub-team is a button.
5. Find the section *Upcoming events*. It lists the next 5 events of the team.

You see names, but no email addresses or phone numbers. A leader, a second or a core team member sees an email address and a phone number on each row. The account badge shows who can sign in, and the date they last did.

## 3. Open the side panel of a teammate

1. Click a name in the *Roster*.
2. Read the panel. It shows the name, the line *Last login*, and the list *Serves on*.
3. Click the *Close* icon at the top of the panel.

You see the panel slide away to the right. If you are a plain member, the panel said *Contact details visible to their team leaders and core members.* The panel also has a button *Full profile*.

## 4. Open your own profile

1. Click your email address at the right end of the header.
2. Read the card at the top. It shows your name, *Email*, *Phone* and *Last login*.
3. Find the section *Serves on*. It lists your teams and your role on each.
4. Find the section *Service timeline*. It is a chart of when you joined each team.

You see your own profile page. Its title is your name. On a phone the header hides your address; open a team page, click your own name, then click *Full profile*.

## 5. Update your contact details

1. Click *Edit* at the top right of the card.
2. Check *First name* and *Last name*.
3. Type your phone number in *Phone*.
4. Leave *Email* as it is. The email address is also how you sign in.
5. Click *Save*.

You see the page again, with the new phone number under your name. Your teams see the new number on their rosters at once.

- To change your email address, follow [Change your email address](../how-to/change-your-email-address.md). A confirmation link goes to the new address first.

## 6. Add your photo

1. Click the person icon, or your photo, beside your name at the top of the card.
2. Read the title of the dialog. It says *Photo —* followed by your name.
3. Click the box *Drop a headshot here (stored as 400×400 JPEG)* and pick your picture.
4. Look at the preview. The site crops the picture to a square.
5. Tick the checkbox that starts with *I confirm this is an appropriate professional photo*.
6. Click *Upload*.

You see the message *Photo saved*. Your photo now shows beside your name on your profile, in the header on every page, and in the side panel your teammates open.

- If *Upload* stays grey, you have not ticked the checkbox.
- To take the photo away later, open the same dialog and click *Remove photo*.

## What you learned

- *My teams* on the *Dashboard* lists your teams and your role on each. A team name opens the team page.
- The *Roster* shows names to every member. Contact details are for the leader, the second and the core team members.
- A name in a roster opens a side panel. *Full profile* opens the whole profile page.
- Your email address in the header opens your own profile. *Edit* changes your contact details.
- The person icon beside your name opens the photo dialog.
