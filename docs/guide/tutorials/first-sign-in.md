# Sign in for the first time

In this tutorial you open your account for the first time and look around. It takes about 10 minutes.

## What you need

- An invitation email from the site. Its subject starts with *Your VolunteerDB account*.
- A phone, tablet or computer with your email and a web browser.
- The email arrived in the last 7 days. The link in it works once, and only for 7 days.

## 1. Open the invitation

1. Open the email with the subject *Your VolunteerDB account*. The parish name can follow.
2. Read it. It says that an account has been created for you.
3. Click the link after the words *Finish setting it up here*.

You see a page with the title *Finish your account setup*. It holds a small card with two password boxes and two checkboxes.

## 2. Choose a password, or skip it

A password is optional. If you skip it, the site emails you a 6-digit code each time you sign in.

1. To set a password, type it in *Password (optional)*.
2. Use at least 15 characters. A phrase of 4 or 5 unrelated words is best.
3. Type the same password again in *Repeat password*.
4. To skip the password, leave both boxes empty.
5. Tick *Keep me signed in* if this is your own device. You then stay signed in for 90 days.
6. Read the short text about personal information.
7. Tick *I agree to keep personal information confidential*.
8. Click *Finish setup and sign in*.

You see a green message that starts with *Welcome!*, and then the *Dashboard*. The site also sends you an email with the subject *Your VolunteerDB account is ready*.

- If a red message says that the two passwords do not match, type them again.
- If a red message says that the password is too short or too well known, choose a different one.
- If a red message says that the link has expired or has already been used, follow [Sign in with an emailed code](../how-to/sign-in-with-a-code.md).

## 3. Look at the Dashboard

The *Dashboard* is the first page after you sign in. What it shows depends on your teams and your role.

1. Find the search box *Find volunteers or teams…* at the top.
2. Find the section *My teams*. Each line is a team you serve on, with your role on it.
3. Find the section *My service*. It shows *Upcoming duties* and *Hours served*.
4. Scroll down to the graph. Each team is a rounded box, and each volunteer is a dot.
5. Scroll to the bottom. Find the section *Guides*. Each link opens a page of this manual.
6. Scroll back up and click a team in the graph.

You see the page of that team. If you are on no team yet, the *Dashboard* has no *My teams* section, and the graph shows nothing for you. In *Guides*, a badge *Tutorial* marks the pages like this one.

## 4. The left side of the header

The header is the coloured bar at the top of every page.

1. Find the parish logo at the left end of the header.
2. Click *Dash*, the word next to the logo.
3. Find the buttons *Teams*, *Volunteers* and *Events* next to *Dash*.
4. Click *Teams*.

You see the *Teams* page, a list of every team in the parish. *Dash* takes you back to the *Dashboard* from any page. On a narrow screen the buttons fold into one *Menu* button with 3 lines.

## 5. The right side of the header

1. Find your email address at the right of the header. A tooltip says *My volunteer profile*.
2. Find the round photo, or the person icon, next to your address. That is your photo.
3. Click the gear icon. A tooltip says *Settings*.
4. Read the menu: *Dark mode*, *Password & sign-in* and *Manual*.
5. Click outside the menu to close it.

You see the header as before. On a phone the address is hidden, and only the photo shows. You add a photo in the next tutorial.

## 6. Sign out and sign in again

1. Click the icon at the far right of the header. A tooltip says *Sign out*.
2. Type your email address in *Email*.
3. If you set a password, type it in *Password (optional)*. If not, leave the box empty.
4. Click *Sign in*.

You see the *Dashboard* again if you typed a password. If you left the box empty, you see a new box, *6-digit code*, and the page says that a code was emailed to you.

5. Open the email with the subject *Your VolunteerDB sign-in code*, followed by the code.
6. Type the code in *6-digit code*. The code works for 10 minutes.
7. Click *Sign in with code*.

You see the *Dashboard*. If the code does not work, click *Resend code* and type the new one.

## What you learned

- The invitation link opens your account once. After that, you sign in with your email address.
- A password is optional. Without one, the site emails you a 6-digit code each time.
- The *Dashboard* shows your teams and your service. *Dash* in the header brings you back to it.
- The gear icon opens *Dark mode*, *Password & sign-in* and *Manual*. The icon at the far right signs you out.
