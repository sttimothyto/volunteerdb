# Your first shift

In this tutorial you find the events of your teams and sign up for a shift. Then you ask for a substitute, cover a shift for a teammate, and put your duties in your own calendar. It takes about 15 minutes.

## What you need

- You are signed in, and you are on at least one team.
- One of your teams has an upcoming event with an open slot. If not, ask your leader to create one.

## 1. Open the Events page

1. Click *Events* in the header.
2. Find the month calendar. Above it are two views, *My duties* and *Whole parish*.
3. Click *Whole parish*.
4. Click *My duties*.
5. Click the name of the next month, at the right of the month name, to move on a month.

You see every event of every team under *Whole parish*, and only the events where you hold a slot under *My duties*. An event you cannot open is plain text, not a link. If you hold no slot yet, the calendar says *Nothing you are signed up for this month.*

## 2. Open an event

1. Scroll down to the table *Upcoming events on your teams*.
2. Read the columns: *When*, *Event*, *Team*, *Location*, *Filled* and *You*.
3. Click the name of an event.

You see the page of the event. Its title is the name of the event. Under the title are the team, a badge with the date, the time, and the location. Below that is the section *Slots*.

## 3. Say whether you can serve

1. Find the card *Can you serve at this event?*
2. Type a short note in *Note (optional)* if you want to.
3. Click *Available*.

You see a green badge, *you said: available*. This is an answer, not a commitment. Your leader reads the answers when they schedule people. The card is on the page only for members of the team, and only before the event.

## 4. Sign up for a slot

1. Find the section *Slots*. Each card is a slot, with a badge such as *0/2*.
2. Click *Sign up* on the slot you want.
3. Read the title of the dialog. It says *Sign up —* followed by the name of the slot.
4. Leave *24 hours before* ticked. You get a reminder email the day before.
5. Tick *7 days before* if you also want a reminder a week before.
6. Click *Sign up*.

You see a green message that says you are on the list. Your name is now on the slot, with the buttons *Need a sub*, *Hand off* and *Withdraw*. The badge on the slot counts you, for example *1/2*. If the event repeats every week, the dialog also offers *Also sign me up for the later weeks of this series*.

## 5. Ask for a substitute

Do this section only if you really cannot serve. *Ask the team* emails every teammate who is not already at the event. Otherwise, read it and go on to section 6.

1. Click *Need a sub* next to your name.
2. Read the dialog *Ask for a substitute*. The first teammate to claim the slot takes it.
3. Type a short note in *Note to the team (optional)*.
4. Click *Ask the team*.

You see a green message that says how many teammates were asked, and the badge *sub wanted* next to your name. You stay on the slot until a teammate takes it. When one does, you get an email with the subject *Substitute found*, followed by the name of the event.

- To take the request back, click *Events* in the header. Under *Your upcoming duties*, click *Withdraw request*.
- To give the slot straight to one teammate, click *Hand off* instead. Do this only after you both agreed.

## 6. Cover a shift for a teammate

1. Click *Events* in the header.
2. Find the section *Teammates need a substitute*. It is on the page only when a teammate has asked.
3. Read the line: the name, the slot, the event, the time, and the note of your teammate.
4. Click *Take this slot*.

You see the message *The slot is yours — thank you!* The event now appears under *Your upcoming duties*, and your teammate gets an email. If the section is not on the page, come back when the email *Substitute needed* reaches you.

## 7. Add your duties to your own calendar

1. Click *Events* in the header.
2. Click *My duties* above the calendar.
3. Click *Add to your calendar*, at the right of the two views.
4. Read the panel *Your duties, in your own calendar*.
5. On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, click *Subscribe in Apple Calendar, Outlook or Thunderbird*. Then confirm in your calendar.
6. For Google Calendar, copy the *Feed address* and follow the words before it in the panel.
7. Click *Close*.

You see your duties in your own calendar. Every slot you sign up for appears there, and stays up to date. Your calendar refreshes about once an hour. The address is private to you: anyone who has it can read your duties. If it gets out, click *Reset the address* in the same panel and subscribe again.

## What you learned

- The *Events* page shows your duties, the month calendar, and the upcoming events of your teams.
- *My duties* shows the events where you hold a slot. *Whole parish* shows every team's events.
- *Available* and *Not available* are answers. *Sign up* is the commitment.
- *Need a sub* asks the whole team. *Hand off* gives the slot to one teammate. *Withdraw* takes you off.
- *Take this slot* covers a shift for a teammate.
- *Add to your calendar* puts your duties in your own calendar, and keeps them up to date.
